Summary

  • Goals:
    1. Form partnerships between TSU and industry in systems-oriented research areas that are critical to the nation’s economic strength
    2. Facilitate transfer of technology and knowledge , and encourage industrial investment in innovation
    3. Provide industry-based training and development for high calibre university graduates.
    4. Establish long-term collaboration between industry and academia
  • Strategies:
    1. Focus on strategic planning with industrial members, attracting new members to their efforts, and developing forums for interaction
    2. Focus on demonstrating successful industrial collaboration and technology transfer results
    3. Putting new technologies into play while attracting new companies and finding new ways of teaming with industry, including generating industrial endowments
  • Confirmed Industrial Partner by President and CEO of Tech & Telecom Engineering Institute, Inc., DC
