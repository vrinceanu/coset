The responsibility of the Internal Advisory Board (IAB) is to monitor the Center’s mission and effectiveness and to provide needed suggestions based upon the metrics and expectations of the Center project. All CAC members defined in section 6.1 will be the basic members of the IAB. The IAB will be chaired by Dr. Ohia, the Provost and Vice-President for Academic Affairs and Research at TSU. The other IAB members will include prominent scientists, distinguished faculty members, and senior administrators from the College of Science and Technology and other colleges at TSU. The IAB will be informed by CAC about major progress resulting from the Center’s activities. Also, the IAB will have a meeting every four months to fulfill its responsibility effectively. The responsibility of the External Advisory Board (EAB) is to identify sustainability challenges and multidisciplinary research opportunities to help the Center achieve its goals. The EAB members will include prominent scientists and engineers from academia, private industry and government laboratories. The EAB is chaired by TSU President Rudley. TSU Provost Ohia has confirmed to act on President’s behalf whenever the President is in the absence. The EAB will be informed by CAC for any major progresses in the Center and the IAB meeting’s decision. All EAB members will be invited to visit CRCN and TSU campus for an EAB meeting once a year to efficiently fulfill EAB’s responsibility.
